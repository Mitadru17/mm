# A mountain journal for Mystic Mountain

The visual direction is an intimate hotel in green Kumaon, presented through honest photographs and an editorial rhythm. The existing property identity and booking paths remain the basis of the product.

## Composition

The opening uses a two-line Cormorant Garamond statement over the real balcony photograph. A dark forest veil keeps the words legible while the landscape remains visible. Hotel identity, a direct stay-planning link and two clearly labelled views provide orientation. The image moves slowly only while visible and enabled.

The arrival chapter pairs a broad statement with readable prose, then separates a large balcony photograph from a smaller timber-room detail. A handwritten annotation acts as an occasional human accent rather than the dominant reading face.

The room carousel retains the existing tested behavior, with a flatter deep-green information panel and square tabs. The destination chapter then changes the pace: green background, sticky copy on wide screens, a large authentic hillside photograph and a smaller open doorway. The original Blender sculpture becomes an optional collection detail, with its heavier viewer loaded only on request.

The experience rail and dining story remain photographic. The gallery becomes a journal with original image ratios and captions below photographs. Booking uses a simple rectangular form, strong labels and the established reviewed-inquiry handoff.

## Type, color and motion

Retain locally hosted Cormorant Garamond for editorial display, DM Sans for reading and controls, and Pinyon Script for occasional annotations. Fluid body type remains 16–18px and form inputs at least 16px. Headlines have independent bounded scales and wrapping rules.

Warm paper, forest green and restrained bronze are drawn from timber, foliage and the existing artwork. Space and image scale create hierarchy; repeated decorative cards are avoided.

Use the existing Motion/Lenis/Embla stack. Entrances use the same `[.22, 1, .36, 1]` easing family, restrained distances and no looping text effects. A selected React Bits BlurText adaptation is confined to the opening. Essential text and booking controls never depend on completing an animation.

Respect system reduced motion and provide an additional persistent on-page reduction control. No artificial particles, unrelated stock property images, invented snowy peaks or fabricated reviews are introduced.
