What is The Bend Club?

The Bend Club is a planned 16-townhome luxury residence club in Bend, Oregon, designed to operate like a micro resort with private residences and concierge programming.

Where is The Bend Club planned?

The concept is centered around Bend's Old Mill District, with access to the Deschutes River, Hayden Homes Amphitheater, dining, golf, and Central Oregon outdoor recreation.
