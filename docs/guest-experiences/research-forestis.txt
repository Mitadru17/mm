Peace as a new luxury

FORESTIS regards itself as a place of retreat surrounded by nature, where time can once again be sensed and filled with emotional value through physical and mental mindfulness towards oneself.

»The combination of the four natural elements with tasteful architecture has created something profound in this place.«

Hosts
Teresa & Stefan

The Suites at FORESTIS are surrounded by dense treetops. Nothing obstructs the view down into the valley, over wooded mountain landscapes or the Dolomites UNESCO World Heritage Site.

Villa

The villa, which can be booked exclusively for up to ten people, is located in the immediate vicinity of the FORESTIS. The historic building, dating from the early 20th century, is sheltered by the surrounding forest: with its own garden and private spa, it offers absolute privacy.
