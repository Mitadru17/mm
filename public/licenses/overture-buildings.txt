Geographic scope: 79.588,29.835 to 79.62,29.87 (longitude, latitude).
Original building geometries retained. No measured heights are present in
these 2,190 records: walls use a stated illustrative 6.4m height with a 0.7m
roof cap and illustrative colours. This is not a survey or a photogrammetric
reconstruction. Trees are procedural illustrations constrained to mapped
woodland, Sentinel forest colour and exclusion buffers around roads/buildings.
